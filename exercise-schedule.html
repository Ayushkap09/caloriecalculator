💡 Workout Tips

Bulk Phase Tips:
  • Focus on progressive overload
  • Eat in a caloric surplus
  • Prioritize compound movements
  • Rest 2-3 mins between heavy sets
Cut Phase Tips:
  • Maintain muscle with resistance training
  • Eat in a caloric deficit
  • Increase cardio frequency
  • Keep protein intake high
