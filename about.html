About Gym Calorie Calculator

Your complete fitness companion for personalized diet plans and workout schedules

Our Mission

We believe that achieving fitness goals doesn't have to be complicated. Our mission is to provide you with accurate, personalized diet and workout plans that work for your lifestyle and objectives.

Whether you're looking to build muscle (bulk) or lose fat (cut), our app gives you the tools and knowledge to succeed.

How It Works

Our calculator uses proven scientific formulas to determine your daily calorie requirements based on your personal data, goals, and activity level.

We then generate customized meal plans and workout schedules tailored to your preferences, whether you follow a vegetarian or non-vegetarian diet.
